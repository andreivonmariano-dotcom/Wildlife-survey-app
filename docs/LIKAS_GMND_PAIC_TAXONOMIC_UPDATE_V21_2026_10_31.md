Date: 31 October 2026. PAIC-only private research reference; no Android or source-rights approval.

Original v2.0 stays locked at 638 named vertebrate candidates, unchanged baseline CSV SHA256 20a45cf58d36baf3e1c4b9de4202c1b33ceab0dbc65c41e01a82501acc590e08 and XLSX SHA256 00df680b32ff566fd289be67f39a847deaae56017f59aecb3a9fe973047e3e0c.

Separate v2.1 has 640 unique named vertebrates (349 birds, 83 mammals, 53 amphibians, 155 reptiles), with 463 descriptive LIKELY and 177 descriptive POSSIBLE candidates. The source-reviewed September 2026 Pitogo et al. taxonomic revision retires the historic southern PAIC assignment of Platymantis dorsalis and recognizes P. jagorii, P. laticeps, P. habagatan. This does not retroactively reidentify historic specimens or resolve each island. https://doi.org/10.11646/zootaxa.5884.2.3.

AviList v2026 (published 20 October 2026) lumps Apus nipalensis into Apus affinis. Preserve original IOC/WBCP name for historical search and do not imply a new locality. https://www.avilist.org/checklist/v2026/.

Four genus-level reference records appear separately without inflating the 640: Platymantis sp. Mt Capistrano (Venturina et al. 2023, https://doi.org/10.56899/152.6A.01), Platymantis sp. 1 and sp. 2 Samar (Diesmos et al. 2026, separate vouchers and advertisement calls, https://doi.org/10.3897/zookeys.1269.173854), and a legacy Sy 2026-1 genus-only Ophiophagus reference (not independently voucher verified). These may ultimately correspond to a named species and are not claims of four undescribed biological species.

Other 2026 taxonomic intelligence is registered conservatively. A 22 October 2026 Gonocephalus paper reports two diagnosable groups but its accessible abstract does not provide sufficient taxon and precise range mapping, so no automatic relabeling of G. semperi: https://doi.org/10.1093/zoolinnean/zlag161. WBCP 2026 committee report describes four new country bird sightings outside Greater Mindanao and notes a swamphen common-name change, but says its 2026 checklist is awaiting formal publication: https://birdwatch.ph/2026/10/01/records-committee-annual-report-2026/. Three 2025 newly named Apomys are Mindoro, not Mindanao: https://www.mammaldiversity.org/releases/diff-changes/2.2/.

Nine separately cited non-core discoveries, not counted in named vertebrates: Thopeutica cassolai, T. intermedia, T. maindai, T. abdita (https://doi.org/10.12976/jib/2026.82.2.1); Platydracus aslaki, P. batman (https://doi.org/10.11646/zootaxa.5881.4.8); Faustabryna piacayetanoae (https://doi.org/10.11646/zootaxa.5869.1.11); Imachra philippinensis (https://doi.org/10.12976/jib/2026.90.1.2); and Lentipes ptasan (published online 2025, in 2026 journal volume, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13052460/).
