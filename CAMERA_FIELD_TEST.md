# Physical field usability checks — v0.5

1. Launch the app. Confirm that **no camera preview appears** and the camera is not running.
2. Start a bird point count. Confirm the **Mark now** and **Open camera** buttons are visible near the top.
3. Tap Mark now. Enter a species and save; verify the timestamp in exported CSV.
4. Start a new record. Mark now, then open camera. Point toward an identifiable bearing, capture the reading and return. Confirm the original marked time remains.
5. Reopen the camera; confirm the picture is **not stretched**, the crosshair appears centered and the azimuth changes as the phone rotates.
6. Press Close without capture. Confirm the survey form and its typed species and distance remain.
7. Complete a survey with zero observations. Confirm it appears in surveys.csv.
8. Repeat with line-transect mode and check calculated perpendicular distances using a handheld compass and measured horizontal distances.
9. Check CSV data after updating from v0.4 Beta. Existing Beta data should remain if Android accepts the update with the cached signing key.
10. Use real survey measurements only after instrument calibration and protocol validation.
