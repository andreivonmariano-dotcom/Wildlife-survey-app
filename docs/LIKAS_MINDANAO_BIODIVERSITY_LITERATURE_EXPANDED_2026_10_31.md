# LIKAS | Expanded Mindanao biodiversity literature review

**Research checkpoint:** 31 October 2026, Asia/Manila. **Status:** Public-safe literature metadata and audit leads only. No changed private workbook, app code, SQLite, APK, or field records. The owner-private row-level WBCP/Sy sources are not included.

## Method and scope
Targeted web searches of publisher/original scientific article pages, taxonomic revisions, MDD, GBIF/Zenodo, Philippine journals, and named Mindanao localities. A separate directly delivered owner-private 29-source annotated literature report and CSV register summarize abstracts/source metadata with review-depth labels. This is **not** a completed systematic PRISMA review and should not be represented as all regional biodiversity studies. The MOBIOS+ raw Darwin Core archive, October 2026 Kampalili supplementary DOC, and entire MDD v2.5 mammal CSV were not extracted in this pass.

## New candidate discovered against existing private mammal register
- **Podogymnura minima** (Erinaceidae; Kitanglad Gymnure), previously **Podogymnura truei minima**, recognized as separate species by Balete et al. 2023, Zootaxa 5228(3), DOI https://doi.org/10.11646/zootaxa.5228.3.2. Original specimen species treatment https://zenodo.org/records/7542097 documents Mt Kitanglad Range, Bukidnon (Mts Dulangdulang, Imbayao and Nangkabulos). MDD current entry https://www.mammaldiversity.org/taxon/1006726/ confirms species and original Mt Kitanglad type locality. Its current IUCN and Philippine DENR assessment are **unverified** and must not be copied automatically from P. truei.
- The owner-private v3.3 mammal rows contain P. truei, P. aureospinula, and P. intermedia but not P. minima. The independent v3.5 private research workbook previously introduced **one** distinct species, Desmalopex leucoptera, moving 99 to 100. This new finding supports **101 possible mammal candidates** only in a proposed future edition, **not** in any modified existing workbook or released app.
