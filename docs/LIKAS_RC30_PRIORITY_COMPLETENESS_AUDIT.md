## Current priority research gaps

Priority 1: zero/one-section species, especially **Dicaeum kampalili** (identification unverified), **Buceros hydrocorax**, **Rhabdotorrhinus leucocephalus**, **Dicaeum retrocinctum**, **Actenoides hombroni**, **Kaloula picta**, **Varanus rasmusseni**, and **Crateromys paulus**. Require original descriptions and island-scoped primary records before filling. The uncommon/unavailable original 1952 `Dicaeum kampalili` diagnosis is not replaced by invented morphology.

Priority 2: all missing conservation sections. National DENR and global IUCN status must be separately sourced with assessment dates and criteria; a lack of a record is not Least Concern. Systematically check original agency publication and edition rather than copying an unversioned label.

Priority 3: natural history, particularly site-specific behavior, activity, food, breeding, microhabitat and quantitative measurements. A photo, nearby fauna or checklist listing does not prove a species trait or site occurrence. Current note counts do not constitute complete ecological accounts.

Priority 4: photograph coverage and quality. Only approved offline licensed photographs with full author/license are displayed, and absence of an eligible photo remains explicit. No photo has been generated to masquerade as research evidence.

## Exact RC30 research additions

- **Pithecophaga jefferyi | Distribution:** Documented island distribution (2014). Exact provenance: Abstract, documented island distribution. Research limit: Historical and published island-level range summary in the 2014 genetic study. The paper sampled 22 individuals, not an exhaustive island occupancy survey; not a current locality polygon. URL: https://doi.org/10.11609/JoTT.o3748.6335-44
- **Penelopides panini | Distribution:** Northwest Panay survey records (2021). Exact provenance: Results, 367 ten-minute point counts over 204.4 km² of the Northwest Panay Peninsula. Research limit: One surveyed Northwest Panay area with 54 encounters, not all Panay, Negros, or the entire species range. No inference about current occurrence at another site. URL: https://doi.org/10.1186/s40657-021-00303-3
- **Rhabdotorrhinus waldeni | Natural history:** Fruit feeding and occasional invertebrates (n.d.). Exact provenance: Habits and Food sections of the hornbill specialist species account. Research limit: Specialist-group synthesis, not a new controlled diet study. The source reports 98% fruit in breeding-season feeding context; no assumption about diets of all populations or life stages. URL: https://iucnhornbills.org/rufous-headed-hornbill/
- **Bubalus mindorensis | Natural history:** Observed social grouping on Mindoro (2015). Exact provenance: Abstract and social-behaviour results; repeated community-based dry-season vantage counts in Mount Iglit–Baco. Research limit: Percentages describe observed dry-season sightings in the studied conservation area during 2006–2011, not a universal year-round social structure or a current census. URL: https://doi.org/10.1017/S0030605314000313
- **Varanus cumingi | Distribution:** Agusan Marsh voucher specimen (2026). Exact provenance: Varanus cumingi treatment; KU 314128, Barangay San Marcos, Municipality of Bunawan, Agusan del Sur. Research limit: One published voucher locality, not an island-wide occupancy estimate. Coordinates and taxon label must not be copied into live observer GPS or inferred current detections. URL: https://doi.org/10.3897/zookeys.1272.167304
- **Acerodon leucotis | Distribution:** Historical Busuanga and Palawan records (2004). Exact provenance: Species treatment Acerodon leucotis, printed p. 280; cited original specimen records by Hoogstraal (1951), Sanborn (1952), and UMMZ material. Research limit: Historic specimen records assembled in 2004. Does not establish current occupation of any island, station, or habitat patch. URL: https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/part/49110
- **Acerodon leucotis | Habitat:** Busuanga forest context in earlier collections (2004). Exact provenance: Acerodon leucotis, printed p. 280, quoting a 1951 Busuanga field observation. Research limit: Habitat around a historical Busuanga locality, not a measured present-day preference, range-wide typical habitat, or proof of tolerance for all disturbed forest. URL: https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/part/49110
- **Acerodon leucotis | Natural history:** Field observations of canopy feeding (2004). Exact provenance: Acerodon leucotis, printed p. 280; Site 12 capture and canopy-feeding observations. Research limit: Field observations from one survey and locality; not a measured general feeding height or verified diet composition. URL: https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/part/49110

Reference policy: the app uses APA 7 author–date claims and source-linked works with fully checked metadata where available. The source index has **306 taxon-publication links covering 199 distinct URLs**, not 306 automatically complete APA reference records. The RC29 catalog had 14 verified entries and RC30 adds four exact-link citations (two DOI studies, one 2004 survey, and one dated-as-`n.d.` specialist species page), totaling **18 verified or author-attributed formatted source records**. The specialist page has no documented publication date.

## Validation and remaining release gates

Evidence pack is applied only to read-only copies of the original research JSON and not to user surveys, original source PDFs, the taxonomic checklist, or saved GPS and media. It is shown in species account, trait comparison, and conservation UI through `Rc30SpeciesEvidence.merge`. Code tests, signed APK build and emulator screenshots are independently checked and recorded in `docs/LIKAS_PROJECT_STATE.md`. At this audit stage the new RC30 source is **not** a phone-validated field release.
