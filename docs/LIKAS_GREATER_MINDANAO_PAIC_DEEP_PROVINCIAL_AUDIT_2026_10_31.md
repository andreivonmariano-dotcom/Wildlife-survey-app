## Source-backed findings
- The previous source-only Greater Mindanao review comprises **399 different taxon keys** (193 bird, 50 amphibian, 156 reptile). These are not a richness estimate, a complete PAIC species list, or validated evidence that every taxon is currently resident. Some are genus/subspecies, introduced, vagrant, marine, historical or uncertain.
- Previously documented **352** legacy Mindanao island labels have **34** source-geographic mismatches (**26** PAIC-scale, **seven** Eastern Visayas/member-island only, **one** offshore historical). A PAIC claim is not an island/province occurrence. Do not silently correct historical records; use a provenance-preserving migration after approval.
- Among **47** additional candidates, the previous semantic review separates 32 positive-looking named-island text statements (still lacking comprehensive independent source validation) from negative, questioned, historical, migrant, introduced and marine qualifiers.
- Published primary research independently supports specific *illustrative locality cases*: *Sarcophanops samarensis* on Samar/Leyte/Bohol versus *S. steerii* on Mindanao and its eastern offshore islands (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8528567/); *Platymantis bayani* type locality in Eastern Samar (https://amphibiansoftheworld.amnh.org/Amphibia/Anura/Ceratobatrachidae/Ceratobatrachinae/Platymantis/Platymantis-bayani); *Levitonius mirus* in Samar/Leyte, with type locality Eastern Samar (https://reptile-database.reptarium.cz/Levitonius/mirus); *Cyrtodactylus mamanwa* confirmed in Dinagat/Unib, with nearby Siargao a candidate requiring its own supporting published occurrence (https://zenodo.org/records/5499432). The 2026 Samar Island Natural Park review independently includes *Platymantis navjoti* on Samar (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12933187/). These examples must not be generalized to other islands/provinces.
- `Ophiophagus` is **genus-level**, not a species-level occurrence in the embedded checklist; the 2024 taxonomic revision requires species-concept reconciliation (https://doi.org/10.5852/ejt.2024.961.2681).

## Provincial geography and evidence
- Reviewed the Philippine Statistics Authority PSGC administrative province directory (https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/provinces), including current 10-digit identifiers. **32 provinces** in the working Greater Mindanao geographic browser: **25** in Mindanao administrative regions including Basilan/Dinagat, plus **six** in Eastern Visayas (Samar, Eastern Samar, Northern Samar, Leyte, Southern Leyte, Biliran) and **Bohol**. Modern administrative areas intersect or include smaller islands and are not equivalents of historical PAIC polygons. The province of **Camiguin** remains a separate peripheral island, while **Sulu and Tawi-Tawi** belong outside Greater Mindanao; Sulu's 2025 administrative transfer to Region IX does not redefine its biogeography (https://psa.gov.ph/content/province-sulu-officially-transferred-region-ix-zamboanga-peninsula).
- A new **source-text lexical screen** finds **502 province-name token matches across 225 national taxon keys**, not 502 validated positive provincial occurrences. Text describing **Bohol, Basilan, Samar, Leyte or Biliran islands cannot prove province-level occurrence**, and political province boundaries often cover multiple islands. Date/rank/negation/history must be checked. All remain candidates until record-level corroboration.
- The existing **11** source-imported Davao provincial rows fall into three categories: **seven** binomial taxon records with the named province in the actual private checklist text (not yet independently confirmed by specimen/source study); **one** trinomial *Trachemys scripta elegans* with source-listed Davao del Sur, requiring species-level taxonomy mapping and introduced-population labeling; and **three** genus-only `Ophiophagus` provincial rows ineligible to be counted as species. **Correction to the truncated initial code review:** the full *Hoplobatrachus chinensis* distribution clause **does name Davao del Sur** among Mindanao provincial localities. Do not claim its imported Davao label lacks support.
- No provincial richness or verified per-province species total is asserted. Preserve no-evidence as **unknown**, not absence.

No mammal PAIC/province records were approved. WBCP/Sy private source text and any derived row-level checklist remain outside public GitHub pending redistribution-rights assessment.
