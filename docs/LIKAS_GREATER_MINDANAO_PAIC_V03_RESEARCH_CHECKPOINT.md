# LIKAS | Greater Mindanao PAIC species checklist, research edition v0.3
**31 October 2026. Research checkpoint, not a final census or approved public checklist.**
**Scope:** PAIC-only geographic checklist. Province/island locality terms remain original evidence metadata; no provincial checklists.

## Audited evidence
- **410** distinct taxon review keys, comprising **399** original private WBCP/Sy birds, amphibians, reptiles and **11** literature-linked mammals. The 410 is *not* an estimate of total PAIC richness or number of native/current resident species.
- **111** source–taxon–location, historical, or exclusion/qualifier links; **55** source entries (46 prior + nine new research, taxonomy and data leads). The 55 include contextual papers and data metadata, not 55 independently completed full-table occurrence reviews.
- **52 of the 399** legacy keys have at least one exact-name paper link, possibly historical, introduced or negative. Exact names must still be matched to current taxon concepts; no record automatically promoted.
- **Four independently checked species-description specimen localities:** *Baletemys kampalili* (type FMNH 208788; https://doi.org/10.1093/jmammal/gyac057), *Tarsomys orientalis* (FMNH 194803; https://doi.org/10.1093/jmammal/gyae051), *Murina hilonghilong* (FMNH 190118; https://doi.org/10.11646/zootaxa.5691.1.1), *Batomys hamiguitan* (FMNH 190163; https://doi.org/10.2988/07-47.1). These type locality statements are geographically informative but not modern presence/abundance measurements.
- **Taxon-name reconciliation:** 2026 Leyte tarsier paper records *Tarsius syrichta* (https://philjournalsci.dost.gov.ph/philippine-tarsier-in-tacloban-city-leyte-the-philippines-amid-urban-sprawl/), current MDD accepted *Carlito syrichta* (https://www.mammaldiversity.org/taxon/1000915/). Preserve both original study name and accepted taxon concept.
- **Island separation:** Campillo et al. (2020; https://doi.org/10.1093/biolinnean/blaa143) distinguish *Sarcophanops steerii* (Mindanao/Dinagat/Siargao samples) from *S. samarensis* (Samar/Leyte/Bohol). Do not extrapolate to all islands.
- **Additional study evidence:** Sanguila et al. 2016 NE Mindanao herps (https://doi.org/10.3897/zookeys.624.9814); Diesmos et al. 2026 Samar island (https://doi.org/10.3897/zookeys.1269.173854); Sanguila et al. 2026 Agusan Marsh (https://doi.org/10.3897/zookeys.1272.167304); Aureo 2025 Bohol herps https://doi.org/10.3329/jbcbm.v10i2.82267 (42 site observations, 54 literature synthesis); Sumilhig et al 2024 Agusan Marsh birds https://doi.org/10.54610/jeseg.v6i2.137 (150 observed incl. 35 migratory); ACB 2026 Mt Hamiguitan avifauna checklist https://doi.org/10.15468/zz6c6h (source observations to 2012, no raw names ingested).
- **Historical source-scoping problem unchanged:** 34 old `island=Mindanao` records require corrections, comprising 26 PAIC-wide, seven separate Eastern Visayas member-island and one historic offshore statement. Still no silent mutation.

## Scientific and release boundaries
Research-only disposition: 248 checklist candidates need original primary locality validation, 71 have special temporal/ecological qualifiers, 42 existing candidates link to articles pending taxon-concept checking, 25 are PAIC-wide broad assertions, seven negated/ambiguous, six geography mismatches, seven contextual literature mammals, four specimen-backed mammal members. No full regional native-resident richness or completeness is asserted. An *unreviewed* locality is not an absence.

MOBIOS+ (https://ipt.pensoft.net/resource?r=mobios_data) advertises 12,813 occurrences, but underlying DwC-A was not downloaded, georeferenced or reused; its CC BY-NC 4.0 license is subject to distribution review. Original private WBCP and Sy statements remain out of public repository. Private 5-tab workbook and per-record CSVs were directly exported in chat; APK, scientific database, field observations, schema27 and UI remain untouched.
