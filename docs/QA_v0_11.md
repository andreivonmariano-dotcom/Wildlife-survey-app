# Wildlife Survey Beta v0.11 test plan

## Three-outcome finish control
- Run a field session, choose Finish Survey. Verify **Complete** and **Incomplete** are ordinary controls; **Not Counted** is red and visually separated.
- Tap Not Counted quickly. It must NOT change survey status.
- Hold Not Counted for two seconds. A separate confirmation form must request a reason.
- Submit a blank or four-character reason. The form must block submission.
- Enter a clear reason, confirm. Check History displays **NOT COUNTED** and the reason.
- Verify archived observations remain saved and audit CSV logs the exclusion.
- Check the dashboard lists Not Counted separately and excludes its detections and stations from normal monitoring totals.
- Check analytical export files exclude Not Counted survey IDs. Raw archival CSVs must still contain them.
- Check other finish outcomes still save and do not unexpectedly exclude records.

## Pause and resume
- Start bird point count. Confirm TOTAL SESSION and ACTIVE EFFORT match before pausing.
- Pause for one minute. TOTAL SESSION should continue, ACTIVE EFFORT and point-count target should stop.
- Resume. Active effort resumes at the correct value, and the visit timer continues toward target.
- Check optional vibration off by default. Enable and verify one haptic signal at 30 seconds, one at overtime.
- Switch apps or lock the device in paused mode. Confirm paused state survives and timestamps remain accurate on reopening.
- Test a GPS-tracked transect. Pause should stop GPS tracking and avoid joining a fictitious straight segment across the break. Resume should restart it.
- Check `effort_pauses.csv`, `surveys.csv` and per-unit pause milliseconds.

## Progress and effort
- Register three stations, visit two and open Station progress. Third should display NOT VISITED.
- Begin, finish early, then complete visits. Check active, incomplete and complete distinctions.
- Collect a camera-trap deployment check with independently verified camera-days, downtime and notes. Export `verified_deployment_effort.csv`.
- Test verified trap-nights and recorder-hours for their applicable methods. The app must never assume checking duration equals functioning device-time.
- Check amphibian listening visits with no species detections are preserved, but the export does not infer definitive absences.
- Inspect GPS-derived path length separately from independently measured line effort.

## Data integrity
- Back up v0.10 using the Export ZIP.
- Install v0.11 over v0.10 without uninstalling.
- Compare survey and record IDs after database migration to schema version 7.
- Export v0.11 and inspect all CSV headers and row counts, including exclusions.
- Run SQLite PRAGMA quick_check through Fieldwork Dashboard. Expect OK.
- Compare methods and station-linked records against handwritten field notes before scientific inference.
