# LIKAS | Greater Mindanao biodiversity literature: source-based evidence review
**Research audit: 31 October 2026, Asia/Manila. Status: selected literature review, not exhaustive or final species/province checklist.**

## Reviewed material and outputs
Owner requested a comprehensive search for Mindanao PAIC and provincial species-checklist literature before accepting geographic species links. A scoped multi-disciplinary review located **46 distinct indexed publications, thesis/repository records or published source datasets** and assessed their geographic applicability. Sources include herpetofauna, birds, mammals, botany, freshwater fishes, marine plants, insects, lichens, bryophytes and island biogeography. Publisher source pages, ZooKeys open full-text tables when indexed, journal/DOI pages, PubMed Central excerpts, DA-NFRDI and university repositories were used with individual access depths declared in the private workbook. A duplicate source was removed. The search is *not* a systematic, PRISMA-complete capture of all Mindanao papers, especially paywalled, locally deposited or non-indexed studies.

A private 8-sheet workbook `LIKAS_Greater_Mindanao_PAIC_Provincial_Literature_Evidence_2026-10-31.xlsx` and searchable Markdown review `LIKAS_Greater_Mindanao_PAIC_Provincial_Literature_Review_2026-10-31.md` were delivered in the research chat, together with bibliography and taxon-evidence CSVs. These include 46 bibliography rows; **105 paper–taxon–location/qualifier statements** (including **nine explicit excluded Samar historical names**), 399 legacy taxon-key crosswalk rows, 32 administrative province coverage rows, primary study-site summaries and a search-method log. **46 of the 399 source taxon keys have an exact-name source evidence link** in this pass; this is not exhaustive vetting of the other 353. Names and taxon concepts may differ. Species-level evidence entries are neither independently counted individuals nor finalized occurrences. The original restricted WBCP/Sy checklist text is not copied into this public repository.

## Critical evidence sources and geographic limits
1. **Sanguila et al. 2016**, *ZooKeys* 624:1–132, https://doi.org/10.3897/zookeys.624.9814. **126 herpetofaunal taxa** from NE Mindanao and adjacent islands. Table 2 marks province/island codes ADN/ADS/CAM/DIN/SIA/MIS/SDN/SDS and H (historical)/N (new): do not convert older records to current confirmed occupancy; Camiguin Sur is independently flagged.
2. **Diesmos et al. 2026**, *ZooKeys* 1269:303–328, https://doi.org/10.3897/zookeys.1269.173854. **79** survey herpetofaunal species in/near Samar Island Natural Park; **104** total species in Samar Island source synthesis. Table 1 distinguishes survey sites versus older citations. Rediscovered `Ramphotyphlops marxi` with vouchers; *Leptobrachium lumadorum*, *Philautus worcesteri*, *Boiga cynodon* reported new island records. Nine older island records explicitly **excluded** from current accepted Samar list must remain negative/rejected evidence, not positive.
3. **Sanguila, Labaja, Bernstein & Brown 2026**, *ZooKeys* 1272:203–251, https://doi.org/10.3897/zookeys.1272.167304. Agusan Marsh (Agusan del Sur) compiled **49** herpetofauna, 14 new site records, four invasive aliens. Table 1 separately codes peat swamp (PS), freshwater swamp (FS), other surveys (OS) and historical (HR), with selected direct-survey and historic-only examples manually reviewed.
4. **Villancio et al. 2021**, *Volant and Small Non-volant Mammals of Dinagat Island*, https://philjournalsci.dost.gov.ph/volant-and-small-non-volant-mammals-of-dinagat-island-philippines/ . Survey reports four new island mammal records (*Harpionycteris whiteheadi*, *Rhinolophus subrufus*, *Pipistrellus javanicus*, *Myotis muricola*). Current 399-key list has no mammals; verify the modern MDD taxon concepts before use.
5. **Decena et al. 2023**, *Philippine Journal of Science* 152(1):1–34, https://doi.org/10.56899/152.01.01. Northeastern Leyte field samples reported **50** herps. This is not the whole island, Southern Leyte or Bohol.
6. **Pitogo, Saavedra & Afuang 2021**, *Philippine Journal of Science* 150(5):1279–1306, https://philjournalsci.dost.gov.ph/amphibians-and-reptiles-of-mount-busa-sarangani-province-a-glimpse-of-the-herpetological-community-of-southern-mindanao-philippines/ . Sarangani Mt Busa survey, **68** taxa (28 amphibians, 40 reptiles).
7. **Venturina et al. 2023**, *Philippine Journal of Science* 152(6A):2031–2048, https://doi.org/10.56899/152.6A.01. Bukidnon Mt Capistrano **32** species, including putative undescribed `Platymantis`; do not promote it to a named species.
8. **Heaney et al. 2006**, *The Mammals of Mt. Kitanglad Nature Park*, https://doi.org/10.3158/0015-0754(2006)186[1:TMOMKN]2.0.CO;2 . Historic Bukidnon survey/museum synthesis documenting 58 mammal species; not contemporary provincial occupancy.
9. **Alonzo et al. 2024**, *Philippine Journal of Fisheries* 31(1):28–34, https://doi.org/10.31398/tpjf/31.1.2023-0011. Maduao River, Maco, Davao de Oro survey: 12 fish species, specific reported new Davao regional records *Bunaka gyrinoides* and *Giuris tolsoni*. These belong in freshwater evidence, not the terrestrial PAIC vertebrate pool.
10. **Caballes et al. 2026**, *Philippine Journal of Science* 155(1):209–224, https://philjournalsci.dost.gov.ph/first-inventory-of-lichens-from-talangisog-forest-misamis-oriental-southern-philippines-including-two-new-country-records-of-foliicolous-species/ . Misamis Oriental: **102** lichen morphospecies (not 102 fully resolved species). A separate 2026 Talangisog understory flora study reports 128 plant species. **Obemio et al. 2026** report 262 pteridophyte taxa along Mt Matutum gradient: https://philjournalsci.dost.gov.ph/altitudinal-zonation-of-pteridophytes-in-mount-matutum-protected-landscape-mmpl-southern-mindanao-philippines/ .
11. **MOBIOS+** published article https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10838081/ and IPT metadata https://ipt.pensoft.net/resource?r=mobios_data report **12,813** occurrence rows from published sources but disagree on some taxon totals; indexed IPT bounding box is global-scale and unusable as an occurrence boundary. The record archive could not be retrieved, so **zero records were imported**. Dataset metadata states CC BY-NC 4.0. Data-level provenance and licensing remain gates.
12. **Synthesis sources:** Tanalgo et al. 2023 bird/mammal review https://doi.org/10.1080/14888386.2023.2210119 (69 papers); Agduma et al. 2023 literature priorities https://doi.org/10.1016/j.regsus.2023.05.003 (99 papers). These give additional bibliography leads, not direct species-level occurrences.

## Scientific status and import gate
A paper's survey count is a *site-and-time-scoped study result*. The 126 NE Mindanao herps, 104 Samar island compilation, 49 Agusan Marsh compilation, 68 Mt Busa survey etc. **must not be added** as independent region richness; studies overlap and use differing concepts. Source review status distinguishes (i) voucher/field survey, (ii) compiled historical list, (iii) island/PAIC broad record, (iv) source-only unverified candidate, (v) genus/unresolved name, (vi) nonnative/vagrant, and (vii) negative/excluded claim. Province names that also name an island do not license administrative-province assignment. No source review changes app SQLite, historical observation or UI.
