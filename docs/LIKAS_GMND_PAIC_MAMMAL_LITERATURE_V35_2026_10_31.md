## Primary literature and dated conservation
- Villancio et al. (2021), *Volant and Small Non-volant Mammals of Dinagat Island, Philippines*, Philippine Journal of Science 150(S1):443–453. Original Dinagat name combinations include `Desmalopex leucopterus` and `Pipistrellus javanicus`; MDD v2.5 accepted names used in the private subset are `Desmalopex leucoptera` and `Alionoctula javanica`. https://philjournalsci.dost.gov.ph/volant-and-small-non-volant-mammals-of-dinagat-island-philippines/
- Jumao-as et al. (2023), published roost account at Timaco Hill, Cotabato City, Mindanao, supports stronger local provenance for **already included** `Acerodon jubatus`. https://philjournalsci.dost.gov.ph/new-distribution-record-of-golden-crowned-flying-fox-roost-pteropodidae-acerodon-jubatus-in-timaco-hill-cotabato-city-bangsamoro-autonomous-region-in-muslim-mindanao-barmm-philippines/
- **DENR DAO 2019-09 primary national listing:** `Acerodon jubatus` **CR**, and `Pteropus leucopterus` **VU** (the latter crosswalks to current MDD `Desmalopex leucoptera`). https://elibrary.bmb.gov.ph/elibrary/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/dao2019-09.pdf
- **IUCN primary assessment:** `Desmalopex leucopterus` **VU A2cd**, assessed 19 July 2020, published 2020. DOI https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2020-3.RLTS.T18731A22081331.en . MDD site displays NE for accepted `D. leucoptera`; this does not erase a dated published VU assessment. Whether the 2020 assessment remains current under IUCN 2026-1 has not been verified.
- MDD species profiles: https://www.mammaldiversity.org/taxon/1004444/ , https://www.mammaldiversity.org/taxon/1005622/ , https://www.mammaldiversity.org/taxon/1004434/ . Distinct national/global categories are not interchangeable.
