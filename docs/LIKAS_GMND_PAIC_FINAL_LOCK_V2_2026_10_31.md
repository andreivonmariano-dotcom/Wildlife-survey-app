# LIKAS Greater Mindanao PAIC | Final, frozen possible-and-likely checklist v2.0
**Frozen 31 October 2026, Philippine time. Dataset ID: LIKAS-GMND-PAIC-POSSIBLE-LIKELY-2026-10-31-LOCK-2.0.**
**Owner-facing status: FINAL REFERENCE SNAPSHOT.** Only PAIC species checklists; province-level list products cancelled.

## Inventory
The immutable private research deliverable contains **638 actual binomial species candidates**: **349 birds, 83 mammals, 51 amphibians and 155 reptiles**. It includes **461 likely** and **177 possible** candidate classifications. The classification is categorical source support, not a statistical detection probability, modelled suitability, an occurrence claim at a field point, or actual PAIC biological richness.

**LIKELY:** The original source or literature connects the taxon to Greater Mindanao or a member island. **POSSIBLE:** A published national checklist explicitly calls the bird widespread in the Philippines without excluding the PAIC, or its range statement questions a plausible PAIC locality. Names missing a species-level epithet (sp., cf., genus only) are not included as species candidates.

## Additions versus privately supplied v1.0 (400 names)
- **162** distinct WBCP 2025 bird binomials added based on their source's explicit *widespread* range statement; no blanket assignment from national inclusion. Common and widespread migrants are tagged seasonal. Widespread statements explicitly restricted to Luzon (e.g. *Otus megalotis*) or indicating probably extirpated *Ciconia episcopus* were not promoted by this rule.
- **One additional questioned bird** (*Caprimulgus jotaka*) admitted as **possible** with a visibly questioned geographic interpretation; five other previously excluded island-specific statements were reinterpreted through the *widespread* rule. An absence on Samar, Bohol or Basilan alone does not rule a species out of the complete PAIC.
- **72 named mammals** added from The Field Museum's Mindanao bat (Rapid Color Guide 49 v1.2) and non-flying mammal (Guide 50 v1.1) lists. Four existing v1.0 mammal names also matched the guide; historical combinations such as *Cervus mariannus* (now *Rusa marianna*), *Urogale everetti* (now *Tupaia everetti*), and *Ptenochirus jagori* (now *Ptenochirus jagorii*) are preserved as cited source names. Existing spelling *Harpionycteris whiteheadi* corrected to MDD v2.5 accepted *Harpyionycteris whiteheadi*.
- **Three herpetofauna** explicitly named in Diesmos et al. 2026 Samar Island research: *Limnonectes leitensis*, *Gonocephalus semperi*, *Lepidodactylus planicauda*.
- Duplicates are collapsed to one binomial display name with source combinations retained. Original introduced, migrant, marine/coastal and historical qualifiers remain visible; no locality, exact identity, or native-resident status fabricated. Only named species are included.
