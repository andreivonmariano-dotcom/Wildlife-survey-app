# v0.14 Field Quality Assurance

## Design math and boundaries
- Choose Birds > Point count > Project A > planned route 2 km and spacing 250 m. Preview must report **9 points**: 0,250,500,750,1000,1250,1500,1750,2000 m.
- Repeat with 100 m (21 points) and 500 m (5 points).
- Try 2.1 km with 250 m. Preview: 9 points ending 2000 m plus 100 m remainder.
- Reject negative/zero distances, fewer than two points, more than 201 points, and lengths greater than 50 km.
- Project is selected before the survey, but no station is demanded on the setup screen.
- Start a survey and inspect Survey Progress. Planned station IDs must all differ and show blank GPS until physically visited.

## On-station workflow and GPS
- Start the survey while outside the first station: point-count timer should **not** start.
- Walk to Point 1 and obtain a fresh GPS fix. Tap Start point 1. Confirm point-count timer starts at 10:00 and station ID/GPS/fix accuracy/time are saved.
- End Point 1. Confirm its timer and count stop. Walk to Point 2, then begin its count. Whole session clock must continue during transit; point-count effort clocks must not include travel.
- Record birds at Point 1 and Point 2 and check their point_count_id differs, even though they share the survey event_id.
- Test poor GPS precision (greater than 75m or older than 30s). The app should warn, offer to wait, or store explicit GPS-missing status on confirmation.
- Finish after fewer than nine stations. Survey must be incomplete if not explicitly marked Not Counted.
- Complete all nine and finish. Survey completion remains subject to time and quality checks.
- Background and reopen during a point. Confirm current planned station, saved coordinates and point timer persist.

## Other methods
- Start 1 km herpetofauna VES under a project. The route length should be saved; no point-count stations should be generated.
- Start bird line transect under a project and check planned vs estimated GPS route length.
- Begin a mammal/other structured survey without a selected station. Use New station here for each visit. Check unique ID and GPS record flag.
- Verify preexisting projects/stations remain unchanged and legacy surveys still display saved observations.

## Export
- Export all archive CSVs and verify `survey_stations.csv`, `point_counts.csv`, `sites.csv` and `surveys.csv`.
- Planned chainage values must be in meters; latitude/longitude must be real observer GPS measurements, never fabricated positions.
- Compare each point-count station GPS against the captured handset location and time.
- Confirm each station's ID remains consistent across sites, survey_stations, point_counts and observations.
- Check Not Counted and incomplete survey flags still separate records from analytical exports.
