# v0.12 Field Test Plan: Region-Aware Species Selection

## Geographic setup
- Start a Bird survey, choose Mindanao in geographic checklist scope. Verify the selected region appears at the top of the species entry.
- Confirm a **Regional suggestions only** checkbox is shown. Switch on. Confirm documented bird examples, such as Philippine Eagle and Mindanao Scops-Owl, are visible.
- Switch off. Confirm the wider offline starter list becomes available, including unexpected species for that location.
- Type a free-text taxon absent from the suggested Mindanao list, then save. Confirm recording is never blocked.
- Test Luzon, Visayas, Palawan, Mindoro, Sulu, All Philippines and Other/uncertain.
- Test Reptiles, Amphibians and Mammals. Groups without reviewed regional data must gracefully fall back to the full list.

## Unidentified taxa
- Test bird → Unidentified bird, Unidentified owl, Unidentified bird call.
- Test reptiles → Unidentified reptile, snake or lizard.
- Test amphibians → Unidentified frog, amphibian call or tadpole.
- Test mammals → Unidentified mammal, bat or mammal sign.
- Check identification_level=unidentified, scientific name blank and group retained in observations.csv.
- Confirm that unidentified bird records cannot become a positive distance candidate merely through distance and initial-position flags.
- Open History and correct an unknown identity to a species. Check an edit trail and revised identification rank. Correct an identified species to 'Unidentified bird' and check the distance candidate flag resets.

## Persistence, exports and science
- Background the app while entering a region-tagged species. Resume and confirm region and draft.
- Export surveys.csv. Check geographic_region_id, geographic_selection_method and regional_taxa_version.
- Export observations.csv. Confirm identification_level, regional_evidence_status and geo_region_at_detection.
- Confirm a lack of a regional tag is recorded as distribution_not_assessed, not absent/out-of-range.
- Run an SDM export and confirm unidentified names are excluded from species-level candidate occurrence data, but retained in archived observations.
- Check a v0.11 → v0.12 install without uninstalling and compare the pre/post record count.
- Confirm Not Counted remains excluded from analytical outputs, and station progress and all three completion outcomes remain functional.

## Boundaries: deferred
- No automatic point-in-polygon location claim in v0.12. Geographic choice is manual.
- The next polygon release needs source/license/version metadata, point accuracy handling, overlap warnings and a separate decision for biological versus administrative geography.
- Neither administrative regions nor named island groups alone justify declaring taxa absent.
