**Research status:** 73 deduplicated source records, comprising 29 preserved from the earlier literature register and 44 new literature leads. This was a targeted wider literature search, **not** a complete PRISMA/systematic review or a complete per-species reconciliation.

## What was actually researched
- Pubmed/journal publisher repositories, Philippine Journal of Science, ZooKeys, Biodiversitas, Journal of Tropical Biodiversity and Biotechnology, university repositories, Natural History research archives, and primary biodiversity paper citations.
- Search terms combined Mindanao, specific watersheds/mountain ranges, the four vertebrate groups, inventories, surveys, specimens, taxonomic revisions and new distribution records.
- **44 new distinct entries:** 29 bird/mammal/herpetofauna/mixed-vertebrate-relevant; 15 flora, invertebrate, marine/freshwater and habitat-context entries. Of 44 new sources, 16 labelled High priority, 22 Medium, six Low.
- All 73 source rows have a source URL. Each new row labels source access (publisher abstract/metadata, indexed research excerpt or reference lead). Original species tables, specimen vouchers and supplementary data were **not** fully reviewed for every paper; some publisher pages were inaccessible.

## Scientific boundaries
The four-group PAIC checklist remains Birds/Reptiles/Amphibians/Mammals. Wider taxa such as Davao coastal seagrass, marine mollusks, Busa orchids, Misamis Oriental Odonata and Mindanao mangrove research are for LIKAS Habitat/Ecology and Research Watch only. Never treat richness of individual sites as PAIC richness or site-level 'new records' as new PAIC species. Distinguish primary survey, voucher, local interview, and compiled regional list. Keep original scientific name and dated IUCN/DENR assessment separately; recent Platymantis, Podogymnura and Ophiophagus concepts require manual reconciliation.
