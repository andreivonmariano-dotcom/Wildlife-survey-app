# LIKAS private research photograph permission: iNaturalist dreidrei

**Recorded:** 28 October 2026.
**Owner statement:** The LIKAS project owner stated in this conversation, "lets still use dreidrei's photos, since that is my account and i own the photographs."
**Authorizing scope:** The project owner authorizes reproduction in LIKAS's **private, noncommercial research Android builds** of photographs that they personally own and uploaded through the iNaturalist account `dreidrei`. This permits a cached identification thumbnail, visible photographer credit, and source link inside LIKAS for offline field reference.
**Copyright remains with the photographer.** This is a **direct, project-specific copyright-holder permission**, not a Creative Commons license, public-domain dedication, assignment, or change to the photo's public iNaturalist license. An "all rights reserved" status must continue to be shown accurately.
**No public redistribution grant:** Public release, app-store distribution, publishing this photo pack, marketing, licensing to third parties, and uses unrelated to LIKAS research require separate authorization. The private research workflow should not package these files into a public GitHub release.
**Ownership safeguard:** Accept a photograph under this permission only if its observation `user.login` equals `dreidrei` **and** the photo attribution identifies the account owner's named photographer in the iNaturalist API. Images attached by other photographers, reposted works, collaborator images, and photos with inconsistent author credits remain excluded until individually cleared.
**Scientific safeguard:** Photographic identification, iNaturalist research-grade status and Philippine checklist membership are independent types of evidence. Do not interpret an iNaturalist occurrence as a LIKAS survey detection or expose obscured coordinates.
**Technical requirements:** Record photo and observation IDs, source URL, attribution, original license, rights basis `owner_direct_private_research_permission`, authorizer account, date, supported taxon name, hash and image file path. Cache only genuinely reviewed JPEG bytes, preserve its image source, never remove the original copyright credit.
**Review and revocation:** New photos require the same author and source screening. Owner can change authorization and remove affected photographs in subsequent builds. Previously distributed offline packages cannot be recalled automatically.
