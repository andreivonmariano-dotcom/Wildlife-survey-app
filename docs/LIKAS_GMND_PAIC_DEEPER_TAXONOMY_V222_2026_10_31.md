# LIKAS | Greater Mindanao PAIC deeper 2025–2026 taxonomy review (private reference v2.2.2)
**31 October 2026 (Asia/Manila). Source-backed possible/likely four-vertebrate-group checklist, not occurrence verification or census.**

## Verified source actions
Source baseline private v2.2.1 had 644 named candidate rows. A deeper 2025–26 comparison to original taxonomic treatments and accepted-name references:
- **Adds `Murina alvarezi`**: Eger et al. (2025), Zootaxa 5691(1):1–44, formal specimen treatment, https://doi.org/10.11646/zootaxa.5691.1.1 and source record https://zenodo.org/records/17358747. Documented Mindanao specimen locality accounts and Bohol in Greater Mindanao; holotype Cebu. Candidate source tier LIKELY, source-status not assumed.
- **Adds `Murina philippinensis`**: same 2025 revision; referred Mindanao specimens FMNH 186818 (Mt Hamiguitan), 195493 (Mt Kampalili), 206220 (Mt Matutum), verified from taxonomic treatment https://zenodo.org/records/17358760. Species originally treated in part as `M. suilla`, so preserve prior original identification rather than reassigning all earlier records.
- **Replaces the Mindanao-region display name `Bronchocela cristatella` with `B. philippina`** under 2026 Supsup et al. revision, https://doi.org/10.1655/HERPMONOGRAPHS-D-24-00003. The old source name and candidate ID remain in the crosswalk; do not count two region species on this evidence.
- **Merges `Limnonectes leitensis` into `L. leytensis`**. Amphibian Species of the World documents the former spelling in Diesmos et al. 2026 under the accepted species `L. leytensis`: https://amphibiansoftheworld.amnh.org/Amphibia/Anura/Dicroglossidae/Dicroglossinae/Limnonectes/Limnonectes-leytensis. No biological species deleted.
- Explicit correction of initial web-search assumption: `Eutropis alcalai` and `E. islamaliit` **already existed** in the 644 rows and are not additions. References: https://doi.org/10.26757/pjsb2020b14012 and https://doi.org/10.3897/zookeys.1269.173854 .
- **Not added**: `Murina baletei` (available reference supports Luzon); new `Gonocephalus` naming and PAIC identity remain unresolved from accessible 22 October 2026 abstract https://doi.org/10.1093/zoolinnean/zlag161 .
