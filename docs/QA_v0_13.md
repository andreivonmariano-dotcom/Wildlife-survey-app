# v0.13 field QA

## Preservation
- Export existing v0.12 data first. Install v0.13 over v0.12 and compare all existing survey and record counts.
- Run SQLite quick_check. Confirm surveys, station visits, exclusions and edit histories.
- Test app backgrounding during partially entered identification and while a photo is pending.

## Geographic assignments
- Register a station with latitude, longitude and known accuracy, e.g. Davao City approx 7.0731 N / 125.6131 E. Open a new survey and tap geographic suggestion. Expect Davao del Sur, PSGC regional classification, then confirm.
- Check Manila (14.5995,120.9842), Puerto Princesa (9.7392,118.7384) and Cebu City (10.3157,123.8854).
- Test a station with missing uncertainty, accuracy >200m, a boundary-edge location or overseas location. The app must refrain from high-confidence automatic suggestions.
- Change the manually selected region after viewing a suggestion and verify it is not silently overridden.
- Check the exported province PSGC and boundary dataset revision.

## Taxonomy
- Open Offline species checklist and inspect the starter size. Import the sample CSV template, verify database count increases and source/version preserved.
- Import malformed CSV and verify the transaction rolls back.
- Check imported names for Birds/Reptiles/Amphibians/Mammals. Restrict by island and province PSGC codes.
- Turn Regional suggestions only off and type an unexpected taxon; it must still save.
- Mark a favorite with ☆, switch taxonomic group and resume, verify favorites stay grouped.
- Record Unidentified bird/reptile/amphibian/mammal. Check no fake binomial is inserted.

## Photos and confidence
- Attach JPG or PNG from phone and observe thumbnail before saving. Save and check History > View photo evidence.
- Switch apps after selecting photo. Reopen, save, and verify photo remains connected to the observation.
- Export ZIP, check actual photos/ files and photo_evidence_index.csv links to record IDs.
- Test >15MB image. The picker must reject it.
- Save Uncertain and Unidentified records and confirm they remain in archival CSV, not species-level SDM or positive bird distance candidates.
- Edit the identification confidence and evidence note through Survey History and check audit record.

## Screen usability
- On a small phone, check Save remains visible, controls have tap targets, and long notes do not obscure Save.
- Expand and collapse bird, mammal and herpetofauna technical sections. Check values are retained when collapsed.
- Test dual clocks, overtime, pause/resume, red Not Counted long hold and recorded effort. These established behaviors must remain operational.
