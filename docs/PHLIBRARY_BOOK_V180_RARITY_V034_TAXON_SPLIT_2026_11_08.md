# PHLibrary Book 01 v1.80 and Discovery Rarity v0.3.4: taxonomic reconciliation

**Accepted owner decision:** 8 November 2026 (Asia/Manila). **Scope:** book and rarity-audit workbook only. No Android app update.

## Accepted taxonomic treatment

1. Replace the earlier regional `Aphantophryne nana` *entry* with **`Oreophryne cf. nana`**, a provisional identification for northeastern Mindanao microhylids, including Mount Lumot material reported by Sanguila et al. (2016). This is a tentative species determination, not a new formal species name and not confirmed Camiguin `Aphantophryne nana`. Retain Camiguin taxon references and old source record in the crosswalk. In the current PAIC book, `Oreophryne cf. nana` is **Unranked** and has **no inherited IUCN/DENR species assessment**. Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5096358/ .
2. Recognize **`Ceyx melanurus`** as **North Philippine Dwarf-kingfisher**, with Samar and Leyte in the Greater Mindanao scope, **IUCN global LC (2024)** and previously assigned PHLibrary **Rare**. BirdLife/IUCN current treatment: https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2024-2.RLTS.T61655451A252381104.en .
3. Add **`Ceyx mindanensis`** as **South Philippine Dwarf-kingfisher**, a separate Philippine-endemic species known from Mindanao and Basilan, **IUCN global LC (2024)**. Assign an editorial, explicitly provisional PHLibrary **Uncommon** tier; no model component scores were reconstructed or recalculated. BirdLife/IUCN: https://datazone.birdlife.org/species/factsheet/south-philippine-dwarf-kingfisher-ceyx-mindanensis . Published nest/foraging evidence from de Leon et al. (2018), *Forktail* 34, 19–21, describes five nests in forest/forest-edge settings and diet that included earthworms and small lizards. https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5c1a9e03f407b482a158da87/t/657ae0e15b042e14e10d997c/1702551778420/Philippine-Dwarf-KF.pdf .
4. The historical Philippine DENR Vulnerable listing refers to the formerly combined `C. melanurus` concept. The new South Philippine species does not silently inherit a modern national threatened-species category. Earlier broad-name observation identifiers are retained pending individual taxonomic allocation. Historical reference: https://www.biodiversity.ph/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/PRLC-Book-vertebrates.pdf .
