## Privacy, coordinates and screen counts
- **2,539 RG observations have obscured/private public geography** and were redacted in all derived CSV and GeoJSON. **45 source rows** contain `private_latitude/private_longitude` fields (33 bird, six herp, six mammal) that were never copied to derivatives. No personally identifying names/observer IDs, original place guesses, private locality text or media URLs exported.
- **9,207 open RG coordinate pairs** retained (birds 6,649; herps 2,284; mammals 274). Reported public positional uncertainty is estimated, not necessarily GPS, and missing values were not treated as 0. Where both public and ordinary accuracy appear, more conservative larger nonnegative value selected.
- **3,478 RG species-binomial open-coordinate candidates** with reported uncertainty <=100 m and no explicitly named outside-PAIC locality keyword flag: birds 2,655, herps 752, mammals 71. **2,493** <=30 m candidates: birds 1,917, herps 528, mammals 48. All **NOT yet spatially intersected with an authoritative Greater Mindanao PAIC polygon**; project membership does not validate region. No landcover/DEM/raster derived values.
- A preliminary **marine/aquatic taxon review** set aside 25 of the <=100m and 13 of the <=30m candidates for separate aquatic/marine method review, leaving **3,453** and **2,480** respectively for terrestrial analyses *after* boundary/coord QA. This is not a definitive habitat ecological-class assignment.
- GeoJSON public open candidate coordinates are in CRS84 [lon,lat] and private; no user/private concealed locations are inferred.

## Taxonomy, elevation and habitat science
- Staging evidence register covers all **723** privately accepted candidate names, with source-to-taxon links from the previous Hijo Forest 122-row and Tinuy-an Falls 20-row original species table crosswalks, matched iNaturalist records and QA categories.
- User requested elevational and habitat preference. **No actual DEM altitudes or habitat/landcover values were produced** because the source CSVs have no elevation column and no approved island polygons, DEM or dated land-cover tiles were supplied or accessible to the processing session. Fields deliberately blank or explicitly NOT ASSESSED; no invented ranges/land-cover categories.
- An included, tested *offline* optional-environment sampler requires (1) authoritative owner-approved WGS84 PAIC polygon; (2) georeferenced Copernicus DEM GLO-30 DSM raster, vertical datum EGM2008; (3) ESA WorldCover 2021 10m raster. It rejects obscured records, unresolved region/boundary buffers, marine review genera and missing uncertainty; samples center/buffer DSM and buffered 2021 landcover class proportions; marks observation-vs-map year mismatch. Script passed controlled **synthetic**, **not real-site**, raster test.
- Species elevation values can be labelled **observed sampled surface elevation** after actual raster matching, not range-wide elevation tolerance. Land-cover proportions at observed locations are **presence-associated cover**, not selection or habitat preference. Proper preference needs use-availability or effort model (Johnson 1980 DOI https://doi.org/10.2307/1937156). Separate source-reported habitat type from environmental raster association. 2021 WorldCover cannot be assumed to represent earlier observations.
- Copernicus https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM ; WorldCover https://esa-worldcover.org/en/data-access ; iNaturalist geo privacy https://help.inaturalist.org/en/support/solutions/articles/151000169938 .
