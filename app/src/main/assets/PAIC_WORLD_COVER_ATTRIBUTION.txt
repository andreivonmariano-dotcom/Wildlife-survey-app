LIKAS PAIC habitat recommendation map: source and attribution

The bundled file paic_worldcover2020_500m.txt is a MODIFIED DERIVATIVE of the
ESA WorldCover 10 m 2020 v100 land-cover classification. The LIKAS derivative
stores the modal land-cover class in approximately 0.005° cells. It is not the
original 10 m map and is not a survey-specific or current habitat observation.

The derivative includes data modifications (modal aggregation, regridding,
geographic extraction, compression, and habitat-class suggestion crosswalk).
Neither ESA nor Copernicus validates or endorses inferred field habitats.
Map suggestions are unconfirmed until field researchers select them.
