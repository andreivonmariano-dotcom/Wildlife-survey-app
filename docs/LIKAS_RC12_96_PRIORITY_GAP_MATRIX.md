## Specialist completion rules

An account receives a completed status only after a named taxonomic reviewer approves identity and diagnostic comparisons, age/sex variation, measurements, published range and habitat, natural-history claims, dated IUCN and independent DENR categories, image rights and correct identification, and documentation of unresolved conflicts. Evidence availability alone cannot approve any field.
